Automatic (follows the sim)

  • Starts as soon as you are in a cloud: first a wide, thin wall of small clouds about 600 m ahead, then the normal clouds beyond it; the first clouds fade in slower
  • Thickness and darkness of every cloud follow the sim's cloud density around you, live, all the time; the number of clouds follows it too
  • Near edges (density changing fast) and in steep climbs or descents, new clouds come closer
  • Clouds stay inside the cloud layer of the current weather, from 150 m above its base to 150 m below its top; nothing is remembered from earlier decks
  • Thin cloud (below 3 % sim density) gets only a very light effect: fewer, fainter clouds and less wing fade
  • Leaving a cloud: the clouds further ahead fade out at once (and the ones above or below you when you leave through the top or base); the last ones close by stay a second, then fade too
  • Wing fade: a stream of small cloud flakes flying along with you, sent backwards over the wings; how many and how dense follow the cloud density, it builds up as the big clouds come around you and fades out right away when you leave
  • Auto (Performance): the number of clouds is set so your frame rate in cloud stays within 10 % of the frame rate just before it, with at least 35 clouds; what it finds is kept for the next cloud
  • Clouds are spread evenly through the whole box around your path: beside, above and below you, without clumps or empty sides; new ones fade in with distance, coming out of the fog instead of popping up
  • Colour: a light grey lit by the sim's own light, darker the denser the cloud and the thicker the deck (from a calibration flight), lighter in the upper part of the deck; Brightness scales it
  • Size, heading and density differ per cloud; drifting with the wind; glow from your lights
