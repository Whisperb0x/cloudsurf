Advanced: what is automatic, fixed settings, debug

Automatic (follows the sim)

  • Starts on entering a cloud, filling the space ahead at once; the first clouds fade in slower
  • Thickness, darkness and number of clouds follow the sim's cloud density
  • Near edges (density changing fast) and in steep climbs or descents, new clouds come closer
  • Clouds stay inside the cloud layer; its real top and base are learned as you fly through them
  • Leaving a cloud: clouds close by stay until passed, the rest fade out (and the ones above or below you when you leave through the top or base)
  • A third or more of the clouds sweep over the wings, small and dense (wing fade); more in thin cloud
  • Size, heading and density differ per cloud; darker at dusk and night; drifting with the wind; glow from your lights
