Advanced: what is automatic, fixed settings, debug

Automatic (follows the sim)

  • Starts on entering a cloud, filling the space ahead at once; the first clouds fade in slower
  • Thickness and darkness of every cloud follow the sim's cloud density around you, live, all the time; the number of clouds follows it too
  • Near edges (density changing fast) and in steep climbs or descents, new clouds come closer
  • Clouds stay inside the cloud layer, from 40 m above its base to 50 m below its top; its real top and base are learned as you fly through them
  • Leaving a cloud: the clouds further ahead fade out at once (and the ones above or below you when you leave through the top or base); the last ones close by stay a second, then fade too
  • Wing fade: a stream of small cloud flakes flying along with you, sent backwards over the wings; how many and how dense follow the cloud density, none outside cloud
  • Auto (Performance): the number of clouds is set so your frame rate in cloud stays within 10 % of the frame rate just before it; what it finds is kept for the next cloud
  • Clouds are spread evenly through the whole box around your path: beside, above and below you, without clumps or empty sides
  • Colour: a neutral light grey lit by the sim's own light, so the sim decides how dark the clouds are; Brightness matches them to the sim's cloud
  • Size, heading and density differ per cloud; drifting with the wind; glow from your lights
